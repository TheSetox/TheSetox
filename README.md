**A friendlier way to keep up with bills and payday.**

[Myrna & Judith](https://myrnaandjudith.com/) is an offline app that helps you track bills, remember due dates, and see what's coming before your next payday. It brings bill tracking, payday countdowns, and local reminders together, with no account required.

SiapKnow's AI agents built it in **Kotlin Multiplatform** and **Compose Multiplatform**, with no manual development.

**[Explore Myrna & Judith](https://myrnaandjudith.com/) · [Get it on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/myrna-judith/id6767477202) · [Get it on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=app.myrnaandjudith)**
